brain

Connect this browser to your Brain.

This extension talks only to the Brain server you run. Nothing is sent anywhere else — not to us, not to analytics.

01Your Brain

Paste the address of your Brain and its access token: the BRAIN_TOKEN your server runs with, or data/token.txt on a local install.

02Page capture

To remember what you read, Chrome needs to let Brain see the pages you visit. Here is exactly what happens with that access:

  • A page is kept only after ~20 seconds of focused reading, or when you copy from it.
  • Banking, email and password-manager sites below are never read. Incognito is never read.
  • Text goes straight to your Brain URL above. You can pause from the toolbar or revoke here any time.

Skip this and Brain still works for saving pages by hand (right-click, Alt+Shift+B) and for ChatGPT, Claude.ai and Gemini.
